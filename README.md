# Customer Behavior & Churn Analysis — E-Commerce Dashboard

## Description
This project analyzes transactional data from an online retailer to identify the behaviors and patterns most associated with customer churn. It gives decision-makers clear, actionable insight into which customer segments are at risk, which product categories drive the most value, and where targeted retention strategies would have the greatest impact. The analysis was built around five core business questions: how purchase frequency and discount usage correlate with retention, which customer segments are most likely to churn, which product categories are most affected by churn, what behaviors signal high churn risk, and what strategies the company can use to retain high-value customers.

## Technologies Used
Tableau for interactive dashboards, KPI cards, cluster analysis, time-series forecasting, and what-if simulation. Data cleaning and feature engineering across 10,000+ records and 15+ fields. Microsoft Word for the final project report.

## Dashboard Overview
The dashboard has two pages. Performance Overview is built for executives who need a fast read on business health: KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, and Discounts, a combo chart comparing Sales vs. Profit by Product Category, and a sales forecast time series. Strategic Opportunities is built for deeper analysis: it segments products into high, mid, and low performers by sales volume and profitability, includes a what-if simulation for profit margins, and a drill-down view by individual product.

## Key Findings
Fashion is the strongest category across sales and profit and should get continued marketing investment. Electronics has a weak profit margin relative to sales volume, which signals a need to revisit pricing and supplier contracts. Discount usage is a real lever: moderate-performing segments respond to targeted promotions without eroding margin. There is a clear split between high-value loyal customers and at-risk segments that are under-engaged or over-discounted.
